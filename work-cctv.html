IP CCTV & NVR Deployment Project

16 cameras, one shop + warehouse, and an owner tired of "camera not recording, sir".

The situation

Old analog setup: blurry DVR, 4 cameras dead, cables chewed by persons unknown (rats, probably). Owner wanted: front door faces clear, billing counter readable, godown covered, and phone viewing because he travels. Budget: average. Not "AI analytics", just "show me who took the parcel".

Funny part: during survey I found one camera pointed lovingly at a wall for two years. Nobody noticed. We fixed that.

Network Topology

[16x IP Cameras] --Cat6--> [16-port PoE Switch]
                                   |
                              [16-ch NVR + 4TB]
                                |         |
                           [Monitor]   [Router -> Phone App (P2P)]

Cameras on their own subnet 192.168.50.0/24, no internet directly — only NVR talks out via P2P cloud. Keeps random cameras off the office LAN and stops them hogging DHCP.

Hardware Used

  • 16× 4MP PoE IP cameras (dome indoors, bullet outdoors, IP66)
  • 1× 16-channel NVR + 4TB surveillance HDD (≈15 days continuous)
  • 1× 16-port PoE switch (120W budget — checked, not guessed)
  • Cat6 runs, PVC conduit outdoors, small UPS for NVR + switch + router
  • 32" monitor at cash desk for live view

What I actually did

Day 1 — survey: walked with owner, marked 16 spots with masking tape. Argued politely against "one camera can see everything" — it can't. Day 2 — cabling: 400m of Cat6, labelled both ends (C1..C16, because future-me forgets). PoE distance check — longest run 72m, fine. Day 3 — mount + focus: height ~3m indoors, angled to catch faces not tops of heads. Set motion zones to skip the ceiling fan — otherwise 500 alerts of "motion detected: fan". Day 4 — NVR: continuous + motion record, overwrite oldest, user accounts (owner admin, staff view-only), phone app QR setup, UPS test by literally pulling the plug.

Results

  • 16/16 cameras online, 15-day retention verified.
  • Billing counter text readable on playback — owner tested with a ₹10 note. Passed.
  • Phone viewing works on mobile data, no port forwarding (P2P).
  • Power cut test: UPS holds NVR + switch + router ~25 min — enough for short cuts.
  • One-page sheet left behind: NVR IP, passwords sealed envelope, how to export clip to USB.

Honest note: night IR range is ~20m, not cinema quality. Told the owner upfront. No complaints since — that's the real metric.
